loupe — themes

Stand-in tiles for building the gallery pattern, in three palettes. The tiles, labels and geometry are identical across all three, so nothing but the colour changes between them. Each tile states its number, role, level and true pixel size; a thumbnail and its enlargement share a hue and a number. Every theme keeps the three levels ordered dark to light, so depth reads without labels. The L-brackets at opposite corners are the cropping check — a missing bracket means something is covering that should be containing. Click any tile for the full-size file.
