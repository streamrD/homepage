loupe — themes

Twelve themes over one engine, in two families. Each is a block of custom properties and nothing else — ground, type, the rail, the environment, the framing round an enlargement, and how much motion the page is allowed to spend. The miniatures are built from those same properties and reproduce each theme's own framing and duotone, so what you see is the theme rather than a picture of it. The three switches are independent chrome axes and combine freely; they change the cards and the links together. Tiles are stand-ins — see the contact sheet, or switch to real photographs.
