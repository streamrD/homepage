Where each one comes from

Hover a card to light up its frame on the negative. Every crop is taken from the full-resolution scan, then squared without distorting, so nothing is stretched.

One caveat worth knowing: iOS ignores your corners on the home-screen icon — it masks apple-touch-icon.png into its own squircle regardless. The radius you pick here is what shows in the browser tab, the bookmark bar and the history list, so it's worth judging at 16px more than at 112.

A is what shipped. design-comps/bake-icons.py cuts it into favicon-16.png, favicon-32.png, favicon-48.png and apple-touch-icon.png — the three favicons rounded with real transparency in the corners, the touch icon left square because iOS masks it into its own squircle anyway.
